Phone: Poly VVX 250, firmware 6.4.6.

Reported symptom: the phone's SIP password was changed on the provisioning side, and afterwards the phone went offline and stopped registering. Putting the old password back brings the phone online again.

What the ticket's attachments show. They are PBX server logs from the night of the change, plus lists of the phones involved. No logs from the phones were attached.
- 125 phones on one customer PBX had their password changed in the same batch. 97 of them went offline and were rolled back to the old password.
- For a phone that went offline, the PBX logged the password change, generated a new configuration file (the configuration service answered 200 OK), and sent the phone a SIP NOTIFY check-sync. The phone answered 200 OK to the NOTIFY.
- On that phone, the check-sync went out about 0.2 seconds before the new configuration file had finished generating. On a phone that kept working, the check-sync went out after the file was ready.
- The password change also started its own configuration generation, and the PBX sent a second check-sync about 20 seconds later. So each phone received two generations and two check-syncs.
- The PBX logged "ILLEGAL ENDING state" after every check-sync NOTIFY, for phones that kept working as well as for phones that went offline. The logs show no other errors or exceptions.
